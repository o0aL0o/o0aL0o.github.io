## Concept

We set out to make a horror game that *stays with you* — not one that startles you once and forgets you. The premise: a family story told through dread and discovery. Where most horror leans on jump scares as a hook, we leaned on **mystery + memory fragments** as the hook.

## Design challenge

Balancing fear and family is genuinely hard. We considered splitting the two into different scenes, but it created a sense of disconnect. Instead, we kept both in the same world — dialogue carries the unsettling tone, props and memory fragments carry the family theme.

> If the dialogue can evoke dread during flashbacks, and the props can carry the family story forward, the game lands on its own terms.

## Controls

- **WASD** — move
- **E / F** — interact / dialogue
- **1 – 9** — use item

## Playthrough

Three main levels, each with its own theme. The player solves puzzles and collects clues to push the story forward. It's a narrative experience — paying attention to the world matters more than twitch skill.

## My contribution

Programming core systems, contributing to game ideas, and designing/animating the 2D character sprites.
