## Role

Mental Health Digital Designer on a mobile-app team. Worked across four task tracks from June to August 2025 — drawing, wireframing, AI generation, and asset handoff to engineering.

## Task 1 — Icons & Backgrounds

Followed the asset list and drew new icons and backgrounds to match the app's design language. Used Figma for system-style icons, and switched to Clip Studio Paint for the more detailed ones.

**Tools:** Figma, Clip Studio Paint

## Task 2 — Feature List & Wireframes

Each new scene needed a feature breakdown and a wireframe before UI design. Listed features in Lark Excel, then moved to wireframes in Figma — labeled boxes indicating type, size, and color. Finished with a feature-spec document.

**Tools:** Figma, Lark Excel, Lark Word

## Task 3 — AI Training & Image Generation

Built a custom LoRA to lock the art style of in-app mini-game backgrounds. Curated and labeled a dataset, then used a Flux-Dex checkpoint with the trained LoRA and a magic-prompt node in ComfyUI to generate themed backgrounds per mini-game.

**Tools:** ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion WebUI, KohyaSS

![Task 3.1](/images/Task3.1.png)

## Task 4 — Asset Management & Export

Exported finished Figma assets, renamed each one descriptively, grouped them by scene, and committed to TortoiseSVN. Verified each asset rendered correctly inside Unity before closing the task.

**Tools:** TortoiseSVN, Unity, Figma
