## Why

Exercise matters. But the modern excuse isn't laziness — it's *time*. Fat Warrior fits a real workout into 10–20 minutes by turning it into a party game.

It's not purely exercise either: there are reaction rounds and competitive rounds in the mix. The goal is participation across ages, with the highest score winning.

![Card UI](/images/cardGame1.jpg)

## Card types

- **Monster** — exercise the pose on the card to earn a point
- **Reaction** — fastest player to copy the pose wins the point
- **Competition** — 1v1 or team, fixed timer, most reps wins
- **Function** — chance cards: extra point, designate a 1v1, halve the next monster's reps

## Flow

Separate function cards from the rest, shuffle the rest, rock-paper-scissors for turn order. Five rounds total. Function cards can be played any time to swing a round.

## What I did

Idea generation, game design, rule writing, card UI design, and running the playtests.
