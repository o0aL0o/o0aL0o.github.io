## Premise

I started with a cyberpunk city idea, but couldn't find the right Fab assets to make it work. After a long browse, jungle / tree / rock / water kits were abundant — so I pivoted.

## World

A central island surrounded by water, with smaller islands to traverse between. Floating rocks in the sky reference the floating-island idea I'd had earlier and add a slightly magical feel. A small village acts as the hub — a familiar "adventure starts here" anchor.

## Quests

Three challenges connect the islands:

- **Parkour** — vertical traversal between floating islands
- **Maze** — navigation through dense jungle
- **Key hunt** — finding the right key to unlock the next area

## What I learned

The biggest lesson: asset-library fit dictates level theme. I'd gone in with cyberpunk in my head, but the jungle kits were *better* — the right tools tend to point you at the right idea. UE5's real-time lighting also made the floating-rocks-vs-ground contrast really sing.
