## Concept

*Homecoming* is an immersive installation that uses physical objects, interactive triggers, and projected visuals to ask a simple question: **what does "home" mean when you carry more than one culture?**

The piece draws on the origins of our group members — Hong Kong, Indonesia, Bangladesh, and Germany — turning each into a small, explorable "room" within the larger installation.

## What I did

I handled the interactive layer end-to-end:

- **Makey Makey wiring** — converting everyday props (paint, wood, photographs) into physical triggers
- **Prop preparation** — sourcing paint, wood, and printed assets
- **Projection triggers** — mapping physical inputs to projected scenes
- **3D models** — for the Bangladesh and Germany room assets

## Process

The project started from scratch. We sketched ideas on paper, narrowed on a four-room format, and built up from there.
